Getting better with every application

Every form it fills teaches it the site: the way from the advert to the form, what the site's boxes mean, what its “next” button is called. The next application through the same site, or any site on the same hiring system, starts from that and needs the engine less. It never learns to submit, sign in or tick a consent box.

Results

Replies from your inbox

Results are only as good as the replies they know about. Replies to your applications are read from your inbox: an interview, an offer or a turn-down moves that role on by itself. Read-only: nothing is marked read, moved or sent.

Weekly digest

One email a week, to you: what went out, who answered, and what's waiting on you. A week with nothing to say sends nothing.
